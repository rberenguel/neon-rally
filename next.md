# Next / Ideas

## Fuel, Tires & Pit Stops

Longer race formats (10+ laps) enabled by resource management mechanics. Cars are F1-style so
this fits naturally.

### Fuel
- Each car has a fuel load that depletes with throttle use (harder on gas = faster burn)
- Running dry causes the engine to cut out — car coasts to a stop
- Fuel level shown on HUD (bar or numeric)

### Tire wear
- Tires degrade over laps; grip (`car.grip`) decreases gradually
- Visual cue: skid marks get wider / more pronounced as grip drops
- Catastrophic wear (blowout) could cause sudden, severe slip spike

### Pit stops
- Dedicated pit lane segment on the track (or a marked box near start/finish)
- Entering the box at low speed triggers a pit stop: timer counts down (refuel + new tires)
- AI cars pit autonomously based on fuel/wear thresholds
- Strategy element: stay out longer on worn tires vs. lose time in the pits

### Race format
- New "Grand Prix" mode in the menu: higher lap count (10–20), fuel and tire wear enabled
- Existing sprint mode unchanged (short races, no resource management)
